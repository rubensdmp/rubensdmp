# Rubens Merlino

**Geologist & software developer** · Data-driven geosciences · Montevideo, Uruguay

I work at the intersection of geology, geophysics and data science, focused on the subsurface of Uruguay. I combine a degree in Geology (Universidad de la República) with 15+ years of experience in software development, data analysis and Big Data environments.

### Current research

- **Cyclostratigraphy from well logs** (M.Sc. in Geosciences, PEDECIBA): spectral analysis of geophysical well logs from the Norte / Paraná Basin to detect orbital forcing and build high-resolution time control.
- **Well-log database**: an integrated, standardized database of DLIS and LAS files from the Paraná Basin.

### Previous work

- **Seaward-dipping reflectors (SDRs)** of the Uruguayan continental margin: seismic interpretation with convolutional neural networks (B.Sc. thesis; ANII–FCE project 2022–2024, Instituto de Ciencias Geológicas, Facultad de Ciencias).
- Results presented at the First EAGE Conference on South Atlantic Offshore Energy Resources (2023), the 51º Congresso Brasileiro de Geologia (2024) and the XI Congreso Uruguayo de Geología (2025).
